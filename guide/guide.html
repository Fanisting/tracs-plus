TRACS+ Course Info

An add-on for the UofT TRACS hiring site. It adds course names, instructors and TA support contacts to the job postings table.

Get started

  1. Pin TRACS+ to the toolbar

    Click the puzzle icon 🧩 at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to TRACS+ Course Info. Clicking the icon opens this guide again.

  2. Open TRACS and sign in

    Sign in so the table shows the Status column for postings you applied to.

    Open TRACS

  3. Search for postings

    Click + Rotman TA next to Search for Postings to list Rotman teaching assistant postings in one click, or pick your own department and click Search for Postings.

What TRACS+ adds

Everything TRACS+ adds carries the TRACS+ badge or a small + mark, so you can tell it apart from TRACS itself.

  • Filter postings by job title lists the job titles in your results with their counts. Pick one to see only those postings. Tick Remember my choice to open with the same title next time.
  • The checkboxes show or hide the new columns and set how instructors are looked up. They sync with your Chrome profile.
  • The last line counts what is listed. Hover over it to see why some courses have no instructor.

New columns, marked +

  • Course Name: hover over it for the course description.
  • Instructor: from the UofT Timetable Builder for the posting's term. Hover over it for each section's instructor.
  • TA Support: the posting's contact email, ready to click.

A tidier table

  • Every posting is on one page, and postings you already submitted stay at the top.
  • Columns that are empty for every listed posting are hidden (Hours too); Dept-App is hidden when every posting shares one.
  • Apply links are red so open postings stand out.

Notes on the application form

Short TRACS+ notes explain the convocation date question and the "best candidate" question. TRACS+ enters "." in the required "best candidate" field when it is empty; write a few sentences instead if the instructor has not already agreed. Nothing is saved until you click Next.

Reading the Instructor column

Names
Instructors in the timetable for the posting's term. In italics with a term underneath: taken from another term (when Use other terms if none is on).
TBA
The course is in the timetable, but no instructor is posted yet.
grad course
Graduate courses (4-digit codes such as RSM2512H) are not in the public timetable.
not in timetable
The timetable does not list this course code.

Privacy

TRACS+ only runs on tracs.utoronto.ca. To find instructors it sends course codes, and nothing about you, to the public UofT Timetable Builder. Results are cached on this computer for a day.

If something looks off

  • "TRACS+ was updated; reload this page": reload the TRACS tab.
  • No new columns: click Search for Postings once.
  • Nothing at all: check that TRACS+ is turned on in chrome://extensions.
